# How to play — the clerk's primer

## Your desk

- **Left / top:** the quay diorama. One ship at a time. Drag to pan, wheel or `+`/`−` to zoom, arrows to nudge. Click any part — crate, hull, bow, masts, flag — to **pin it as evidence**. Red ink means you found the lie.
- **Right / bottom:** the papers. `Tab` cycles **Manifest → Registry → Rulebook → Tide Table**.
- **Pinned bottom:** four stamps — **Approve `A`**, **Deny `D`**, **Hold & Search `H`**, **Fine `F`**.
- **Top:** shift clock, fees, strikes (3 blots), faction pips, sound toggle (`M`).

## Every shift

1. The bell rings; a ship docks. Read the **manifest**: goods, declared trade + pattern, counts, masses, seal, flag, tide window.
2. Look at the **drawing**. Compare. The lie lives in exactly one place:
   - a seal drawn in the wrong hand (heavy vs light stroke),
   - a crate wearing another trade's colour and stripe pattern,
   - tally marks that disagree with the declared count,
   - a silhouette that disagrees with the **Registry** (use *Overlay silhouette*),
   - a bell that rang outside the declared **tide window**,
   - — or something hidden: a tarped crate, forged ink, a scraped hull line.
3. Cross-check with tools: **Magnifier** loupe, **Brass Scale** (hover a crate: drawn vs manifest mass), **Ink Reagent** (wets the page — forged ink bleeds), **Lamp** (reveals erased-and-redrawn lines). Tools unlock with rank.
4. If a tarp looks wrong, stamp **Hold & Search** (−45 s). Tarps lift; undeclared goods print on the manifest report. Searching an honest captain costs the Guild's goodwill.
5. Stamp the verdict:
   - **Honest ship** → `A` (+fee). Denying or fining honest paper = strike + furious factions.
   - **Forged ship** → `D` (fee ×2) or `F` (fee ×3, only when the rule allows fines). Approving a forgery = strike, and the underworld notices.
6. **Pin before you stamp** — +3¢ per correct pin, and "caught red-handed" makes a better verdict card.
7. Three strikes end the shift: fees forfeit, career continues. Survive all ships (or the clock) and a clean ledger pays +50¢ × ships.

## The rulebook grows

Chapter by chapter the Harbor Master unseals new rules — seals (ch. 1), trade colours (ch. 2), counts & mass (ch. 3), registry geometry (ch. 4), flags & tides (ch. 5), hidden & ink (ch. 6). Escalation is additive, never faster: older rules stay in force forever.

## Seeds & dailies

**Daily** plays the same six ships for everyone on Earth that day (`customs-v1:<date>`). **Seed…** lets you replay any fate by string — the same seed always draws the same shift. The verdict card (shift end) renders the best catch with the lie circled in red ink; download the PNG or copy the seed.

## Reading for screen readers

Every visual claim on the papers carries a text description (seal stroke weight, drawn cargo, colours and patterns) in the accessibility labels; a live region announces the drawn state of each ship as it docks. Keyboard covers the whole desk: `A/D/H/F`, `Tab`, arrows, `+`/`−`, `M`.
